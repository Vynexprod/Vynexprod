Privacy Policy

Playnest for Android - Last updated: October 13, 2026
The short version: we do not have accounts, we do not run a server that stores your data, and we do not sell your data. Your game progress stays on your phone. The only outside company that handles data is Google, which provides the ads (Google AdMob).

1. Who we are

Playnest ("the app", "we", "us") is published by Vynex. Contact: supportvynexteam@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your device: your game progress (levels, stars, scores, in-game coins and unlocks), your settings (sound, vibration), and the age group you choose on the first launch (see section 5). This information is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and use information such as: your device's advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and operating-system information, app information, and how you interact with ads. Google may use this information for serving ads, measuring performance, preventing fraud and abuse, and - where you have agreed and the law allows - personalising ads.

Google's use of this information is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. On Android you can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in Settings > Google > Ads (the exact path depends on your device). We do not control what Google does with this data.

4. Consent in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

If you are in these regions the app asks for your consent for personalised ads using Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform). You can review or change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Ad privacy options.

5. Age and children

On first launch the app asks for your birth month and year. We use it only to choose the right ad settings: it is converted into an age group on your device, the date itself is not stored, and nothing is sent to us. If you tell us you are under 13 we treat the app as child-directed for advertising purposes: no personalised ads, family-appropriate ad content only, and no use of your advertising ID for advertising. If you tell us you are under 16 we turn off personalised ads.

The app is not designed to collect personal information from anyone, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not collect any, there is nothing for us to delete; if you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

6. Purchases

If the app offers purchases (for example, removing ads), they are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

7. Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side. To delete the data stored on your device, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings. Data held by Google as part of ads is handled under Google's policies linked above.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or California law) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Since we do not hold your personal data we cannot provide or delete it, but you can contact us with any question and we will answer. For data processed by Google, see Google's privacy tools at myaccount.google.com.

9. Security

Your data stays on your device, protected by your phone's own security. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data (for example by adding new features or services) we will update this page and the date above. Please check it from time to time.

11. Contact

Email: supportvynexteam@gmail.com

This policy was written for the app as it works today. If you add new features that collect data (accounts, analytics, online leaderboards), it must be updated.
